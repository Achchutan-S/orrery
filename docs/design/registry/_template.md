## Brief

Keep this section to one page. Each line is a decision with its reason ([how to write one](../creative-process.md#writing-a-brief)).

1. **Subject and thesis.** What it is about, and the one claim it makes.
2. **Audience.** Who it is for and what they already know.
3. **The one thing.** What a visitor should feel or understand when they leave.
4. **References.** Each with a link and one sentence on what is borrowed.
5. **Must avoid.** What this must not look or behave like.
6. **Typography.** Faces, sizes, hierarchy; where the files come from and their licence.
7. **Colour.** Palette and what each colour is for; light, dark or both.
8. **Composition.** Grid, margins, density, how a screen is arranged.
9. **Imagery.** Photographs, illustration, diagrams, generated scenes; sources and licences.
10. **Motion.** What moves, why, and what a reduced-motion visitor sees instead.
11. **Sound.** What is heard, why, or "none".
12. **Phone, tablet, desktop.** What changes at each, not only what shrinks.
13. **Beacon.** Where it sits; whether its default look fits.
14. **Acceptance statements.** Five to ten, each answerable yes or no by looking.
    - [ ] …

**The slice.** Which screen or sequence is built first, and why it is the riskiest.
