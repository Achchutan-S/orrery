# Platform identity — direction record

Experience id: the platform's own pages (404), and the starting point of `index` and the Beacon · Status: published before the creative process existed (task 7, 1 November 2026); reconstructed from [platform-identity.md](../platform-identity.md), which remains the full design note

## Brief (reconstructed)

1. **Subject and thesis.** One engineer's growing body of work. The platform owns almost no visible surface, so its identity must sit quietly under any experience and still be recognisable on the few pages it owns.
2. **Audience.** Senior engineers and recruiters who judge craft quickly; curious visitors who browse.
3. **The one thing.** Every entry tells you what opening it costs.
4. **References.** A maker's catalogue of instruments; a type-specimen book (for direction B). No links were recorded.
5. **Must avoid.** Decorative statistics; a steampunk reading of green and brass; textures and ornament.
6. **Typography.** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (SIL OFL), self-hosted, 400 and 700: a legibility face, because access is the one thing the platform owns everywhere.
7. **Colour.** Mineral greens and graphite, used flatly; brass only for the focus ring.
8. **Composition.** A hanging index: titles left, description and weight line right; one column on phones.
9. **Imagery.** None.
10. **Motion.** None beyond a 160 ms duration token, zero under reduced motion.
11. **Sound.** None.
12. **Phone, tablet, desktop.** One column to two.
13. **Beacon.** A fixed pill at the inline end, bottom; self-contained colours so it reads on any background.
14. **Acceptance statements.** None were written at the time.

## Directions considered

| Direction | The idea | Outcome | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Ephemeris | The index as an astronomical table: rows of precise figures, one static orrery diagram | rejected | The figures would be invented; a table of decorative statistics; lands on the broadsheet and hairline default |
| B. Specimen sheet | Each entry's title in that experience's own typeface, the platform's type almost invisible | deferred, then used | Right but premature: no experience had a typeface yet. The Index later took this idea as its `headline` families (ADR-019) |
| C. Instrument maker's catalogue | Plain, exact, honest about what each piece costs to open; the focus ring as the one signature | **selected** | The only direction whose memorable idea came from real data the platform has and from its real responsibility |
